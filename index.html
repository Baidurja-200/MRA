Nykaa Decision Support System Documentation

System Objective: The Nykaa Insight Decision Support System (DSS) is an enterprise analytical web application designed for executive leadership, marketing strategists, category managers, and supply chain operators. It unifies omnichannel sales data, customer behavior metrics, marketing performance, and inventory health into a high-visibility, crisp analytical dashboard.


Data Foundation: Fully integrated with the official multi-tab Nykaa dataset, incorporating Date Dimensions (FY25-26), Geographic Warehouses, Physical Store Networks, 40+ Brands, 500 Product SKUs, 100 Marketing Campaigns, and 5,000 Customer Demographics.
